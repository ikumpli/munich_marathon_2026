🏁 Race Day Strategy
  • Target: Sub 4:00 (avg 5:40 min/km).
  • Split strategy: Negative split — first half slightly conservative (~2:02), second half push (~1:58).
  • First 5k: Resist the crowd, run by feel not GPS.
  • Fuel: Gel every 30–40 min from km 10 onward.
  • Carb load: 48 h pre-race. Sleep well 2 nights before.
  • Warm-up: 10 min easy jog + drills, 30 min before gun.
⚡ Key Session Types
  • 400m intervals (Base, W1–3): 6×400m with 2 min rest — first 3 reps at constant rhythm (~4:45–4:50/km), last 3 progressive getting faster each rep. Add 2 more reps in W3 when ready.
  • VO2 intervals (Build+): 6×1k or 5×1200m at 3–5k effort with 90 s–2 min recovery.
  • Tempo / Threshold: 20–40 min continuous at comfortably hard pace (4:50–5:20/km).
  • MP runs: 8–16k blocks at target 5:40 pace.
  • Long run: From W13 include MP segments (final 8–16k at MP).
🛡️ Injury Prevention
  • Never increase weekly km > 10 % in one step.
  • Step-back week every 4th week (−15 % volume).
  • 2×/week strength: glutes, core, single-leg stability.
  • If resting HR is elevated or legs feel heavy → swap quality for easy miles.
  • Swim as low-impact cross-training when legs need a break.

General sports-nutrition guidelines scaled to your profile (74 kg, marathon debutant, sub-4:00 goal) — not personalized medical or dietitian advice. Adjust to what actually sits well with your stomach in training.
🍚 Daily Fueling Targets (~74 kg)
  • Rest / easy days: ~3–5 g/kg carbs (220–370 g) · 1.6–2.0 g/kg protein (120–150 g).
  • Quality or long-run days: ~6–8 g/kg carbs (440–590 g) — same protein target.
  • Peak Build/Specific weeks (20k+ long runs): up to 8–10 g/kg carbs (590–740 g).
  • Fat fills the rest of your calorie needs — favor olive oil, nuts, avocado, oily fish.
  • Spread protein across meals (~25–35 g each) rather than one big serving.
💧 Hydration & Electrolytes
  • Baseline: ~35–45 ml/kg/day (~2.6–3.3 L), more on hot training days.
  • During runs >60–75 min: 400–800 ml/hour depending on heat and sweat rate.
  • Add electrolytes (sodium ~300–700 mg/hour) on hot, sweaty sessions — Jul/Aug heat matters more than October race day.
  • After a hard or hot session, rehydrate with ~125–150% of the fluid you lost.
🌅 Before a Morning Run
  • Easy runs < 60 min: fine fasted, or just water/coffee — no need to eat.
  • Quality sessions or long runs: eat 60–90 min before, ~0.5–1.5 g/kg carbs (40–110 g), low fat/fiber/protein to avoid GI trouble.
  • Good options: oatmeal + banana + honey, toast + jam, rice cakes, a ripe banana.
  • Sip 300–500 ml water in the hour before departure.
🔁 After a Run — Recovery Window
  • Within 30–60 min: carbs + protein at roughly a 3:1–4:1 ratio — ~1.0–1.2 g/kg carbs (75–90 g) + ~0.3 g/kg protein (~20–25 g).
  • Easy options: chocolate milk + banana, yogurt + granola + fruit, rice + eggs or chicken.
  • Rehydrate steadily rather than chugging all at once.
  • The harder or longer the session, the more this window matters — don't skip it after long runs or quality days.
🌙 Evening / Dinner, Day-to-Day
  • Balanced plate: palm of protein, fist of complex carbs (rice, potatoes, pasta, quinoa), plenty of veg, a thumb of healthy fat.
  • Night before a quality session or long run: lean slightly higher-carb, keep it familiar and easy to digest — not the night to try something new or very spicy/fatty.
  • Avoid heavy, fatty meals within 2–3 h of bedtime for sleep quality; keep hydrating through the evening rather than a big volume right before bed.
🏃‍♂️ Long-Run & In-Run Fueling
  • Runs > 75–90 min: fuel during — start ~30–45 min in, then small doses every 15–20 min.
  • Target ~30–60 g carbs/hour (gels, chews, sports drink), scaling toward 60–90 g/hour for your longest runs (24–30k) if your gut tolerates it.
  • Rule of thumb: nothing new on race day — test every gel/drink/timing in training first, especially on the Sunday long runs.
🏁 Race Day Nutrition — Munich Marathon, Oct 11
3 Days Before
Carb-load to ~8–10 g/kg/day (590–740 g). Keep fat/fiber moderate to avoid GI issues — this is about topping up glycogen, not stuffing yourself.
Race Morning
2–3 h before the gun: familiar, carb-rich, low-fat/fiber meal, ~1–3 g/kg carbs (75–220 g) — e.g. white bread/bagel + honey, oatmeal + banana. Optional small gel/banana 15–30 min before start.
