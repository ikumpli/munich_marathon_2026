🏁 Race Day Strategy
  • Target: Sub 4:00 (avg 5:40 min/km).
  • Split strategy: Negative split — first half slightly conservative (~2:02), second half push (~1:58).
  • First 5k: Resist the crowd, run by feel not GPS.
  • Fuel: Gel every 30–40 min from km 10 onward.
  • Carb load: 48 h pre-race. Sleep well 2 nights before.
  • Warm-up: 10 min easy jog + drills, 30 min before gun.
⚡ Key Session Types
  • 400m intervals (Base, W1–3): 6×400m with 2 min rest — first 3 reps at constant rhythm (~4:45–4:50/km), last 3 progressive getting faster each rep. Add 2 more reps in W3 when ready.
  • VO2 intervals (Build+): 6×1k or 5×1200m at 3–5k effort with 90 s–2 min recovery.
  • Tempo / Threshold: 20–40 min continuous at comfortably hard pace (4:50–5:20/km).
  • MP runs: 8–16k blocks at target 5:40 pace.
  • Long run: From W13 include MP segments (final 8–16k at MP).
🛡️ Injury Prevention
  • Never increase weekly km > 10 % in one step.
  • Step-back week every 4th week (−15 % volume).
  • 2×/week strength: glutes, core, single-leg stability.
  • If resting HR is elevated or legs feel heavy → swap quality for easy miles.
  • Swim as low-impact cross-training when legs need a break.
